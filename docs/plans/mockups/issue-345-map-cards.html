The map's cards: facts at a tap, actions on right-click

A clickable mock-up for #345. The plan, the research and the order of work are in docs/plans/issue-345-map-cards.md.

Tap a frame, hold it for half a second, or right-click it on a desktop. Reset starts a frame again. The switches at the bottom right change every frame.

Revised (2026-10-21, after the user's second review)

The Cards switch at the bottom right shows each version on every frame: As approved (the morning), Revision 1 (its own controls, 08bd488), Revision 2 (the app's controls) and Revision 3 (Revision 1's look, with Back in words, a line only where it adds what the name doesn't say, and compact on desktops) Revision 4 (Revision 3 with an icon on every row, a hover, and a line on every item of a list) and Revision 5 (the default: Revision 4 with compact cards on desktops, the point's edits as buttons in rows there, End here with a flag, the point's row lit in the list, and every control doing what it says). Nothing is overwritten.

  • The app's own controls, nothing new: rows that open the next step are the ways to begin's tiles; actions in a group are the ⋯ menu's items; the main action is Make 3 loops' button; switches are Distance · Time; chips are the lengths'; Back has words. The CSS is a copy of the app's, with the same class names (section 0 shows them side by side).
  • One main thing stands out: the pill at your finger, a filled button (Edit this stretch, Avoid it), or a point's At this point switch.
  • Start here and End here on a point and on a spot, each saying what it does. End, not finish.
  • What's here › on every card, for the map's details.
  • Only what can be done now: no route, only Start a route here and Round trip; a spot by the line, Add a point here; the route's view, the planner's "You're not editing" note with Edit.
  • Undo and redo stay while a card is open. The switch at the bottom right shows the cards as approved.

  • The whole route on the route's card (#90). A: not there, ⋯ keeps it, and #90 closes. B, chosen: one row, The whole route ›, opening what ⋯ has, in its groups and order. The card stays about its spot, Clear route keeps its Undo, and nothing is listed twice. C: #90's four rows, as filed.
  • The start while drawing (#334). A, chosen: Back to start comes first on the start's card. B: a tap on the start closes the loop, with Undo. A tap on any other point opens its card, so the start would behave differently, and a loop by mistake would be easy.

Decided with the user (2026-10-21)

  • A tap on the empty map adds a point, as today.
  • A tap on the route, a point or a place's icon shows its facts.
  • A long press or a right-click shows what's there and what you can do.
  • The action sits at your finger: a pill on a phone, the first row on a desktop.
  • A stretch's settings are one step away, behind Edit this part.
  • A card shows three facts for your sport. More shows the rest.
  • Not right? on every card opens OpenStreetMap's note page or its editor.
  • Share sends a link to the spot. The coordinates are in More.

New for the cards

The app has nothing like these. Each needs your OK.

  • The thing's own mark (card-mark): the point's marker, the stretch's line, a spot's pin, a place's icon, left of its name.
  • A missing number (sum-item miss): a dashed box that says so, as #345 decided.
  • A menu that sits in a card (menu in-card): the ⋯ menu's items and headings without its popup.
  • The pill at the finger on the map, as #345 decided.

Fixes the cards need

  • The route's numbers' labels wrap at 12.5 px instead of 11 px cut with "…" (the accessibility baseline). Drawn here already; it lands in the controls' own Fix PR (#356), in the peek too.

Not decided

  • Icons in menu items: black (the ⋯ menu now) or green (the start's rows). The cards follow whichever you pick (#356).
