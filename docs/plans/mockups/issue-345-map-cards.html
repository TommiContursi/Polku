The map's cards: facts at a tap, actions on right-click

A clickable mock-up for #345, with its plan in docs/plans/issue-345-map-cards.md (the research, what's reused, the order of work). Tap, click, hold 0.5 s, and on desktops right-click; each frame keeps its own state, and Reset starts it again. The switches at the bottom right apply to every frame: the size (390, 360, 200 %), Finnish (rough, only to test the room words take), long names, and dark.

Decided with the user (2026-10-21)

  • A tap on the empty map adds a point, as today: drawing stays one tap a point, with Undo on the toolbox.
  • A tap on the route or a point shows its facts; what you can do with it is one step away.
  • Right-click, or a long press on a phone, shows actions and what's there, since a phone has no hover. On a desktop the hover tip stays.
  • The pill at the spot: on a phone, + Add a point here (or + Add to my route) sits just above the finger, and the facts go in the planner's peek, as points, stretches and warnings already do (D and C of #233). On a desktop the card opens at the pointer, its action first, like a right-click menu.
  • A stretch's settings are one step away (a change to D): facts first, Edit this part › for D's settings as approved; a right-click or a long press on the route opens them directly.

1 · A tap on the route (phones)

The pin marks the spot, and + Add a point here sits right above it. The peek holds the spot's facts (where on the route, the height and gradient, the way and its name, when you'd be there), the stretch it's on with its warnings, and What's under › for the map's own card of the trail under the line. The toolbox steps aside while a card is open (D). A tap on the map, Back or ✕ closes the card and adds nothing.

2 · A long press on the map (phones)

Hold still for half a second: a ring fills under the finger (a phone's web page can't buzz on an iPhone, so it's shown, not felt). A move of 10 px, or a second finger, is the map's: it pans or zooms, and nothing opens. The card is short, in the order decided: Add to my route at the finger, one line of what's there with More ›, the route's actions, and small at the bottom the coordinates with Copy and Weather here ›.

3 · A tap on a point (phones)

D's point card: its name and when you're there first, then the everyday actions by name. Move is the way without dragging (WCAG 2.2's 2.5.7): tap where it goes. Dragging the marker stays.

4 · Desktops: at the pointer

The planner beside the map is drawn simply here, on purpose: only what the cards need; the real panel stays as it is (the calmer planner's plan keeps it). A click on the route, and a right-click anywhere, open a card at the pointer, its top-left corner at the click and its action first, as a right-click menu does; near an edge it opens the other way. The hover tip stays. The keyboard does the same: Tab to the map, the arrows move the crosshair, Enter clicks there, Shift+F10 (or the menu key) right-clicks there.

5 · The worst case

360 × 740 in Finnish with long names, and 200 % zoom (a 390 phone's 195 × 422 CSS pixels). The switches turn any frame into these; here they're fixed.
