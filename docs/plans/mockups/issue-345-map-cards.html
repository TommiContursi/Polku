The map's cards: facts at a tap, actions on right-click

A clickable mock-up for #345. The plan, the research and the order of work are in docs/plans/issue-345-map-cards.md.

Tap a frame, hold it for half a second, or right-click it on a desktop. Reset starts a frame again. The switches at the bottom right change every frame.

Decided with the user (2026-10-21)

  • A tap on the empty map adds a point, as today.
  • A tap on the route, a point or a place's icon shows its facts.
  • A long press or a right-click shows what's there and what you can do.
  • The action sits at your finger: a pill on a phone, the first row on a desktop.
  • A stretch's settings are one step away, behind Edit this part.
  • A card shows three facts for your sport. More shows the rest.
  • Not right? on every card opens OpenStreetMap's note page or its editor.
  • Share sends a link to the spot. The coordinates are in More.

1 · A tap on the route

A pin marks the spot, with + Add a point here just above it. The facts go in the peek at the bottom.

2 · A long press on the map

Hold still for half a second. A ring fills under your finger.

3 · A place on the map

A tap on a place's icon shows it, even while you draw.

4 · A tap on a point

When you get there, and what you can do with it.

5 · Desktops

The card opens at the pointer, its action first.

The planner beside the map is drawn simply here, on purpose. The real panel stays as it is.

6 · Another sport, missing data, the worst case

Each frame here has its switch fixed.
