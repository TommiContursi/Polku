What’s drawn from the app, and what isn’t

  • From main at 9849191: the numbers, the key facts, the route’s list (its rows, parts, waits and warnings as Parts 21 built them), Today’s Check and Go panes (the app’s own DOM), the profiles (each route’s own heights and way types from the app’s analysis), On the route’s lengths, Go’s weather, sun and time on the way.
  • Illustrative: the hours of Best time to leave and the three named departures (the build works them out from the forecast along the route, as #273 says), the crossing window’s waves, Arrive by’s times, and the search’s results in the sheet (the top bar’s, for “Pyynikin näkötorni”, are real).
  • Left out: the map, the route on it and the map’s buttons (stand-ins); the phone’s toolbar and the desktop’s, drawn simply (#594’s D stays); Reorder; the cards of a waypoint and a leg (#690’s round); the bottom dock (as the side dock, in two columns, #761’s PR 9); motion (none is drawn: each new motion is decided with /animate when it’s built).

A tool lives where you work with it (decided 2026-11-06): the toolbar keeps every tool it has, and as it narrows Take it along goes to More first; the list’s foot keeps Reorder, Reverse and Back to start. Green means you can tap it: a value, a fold, a way back, or the one main action.
