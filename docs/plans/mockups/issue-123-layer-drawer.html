A quick layer drawer, before the full Layers panel

Mock-up for #123, 2026-10-16, reviewed with design skills for coding agents and changed after the first round of answers. Not wired to the app: the map behind each frame is a screenshot of Polku; the drawer, buttons and panels are drawn in Polku's own colours and icons. The plan is docs/plans/issue-123-layer-drawer.md.

Try it: tap maps and tiles; › on a tile opens its settings; Edit changes the tiles; More opens all layers. On a phone frame, drag the sheet down to close it or up for all layers; Layers opens it again.

Decided (2026-10-16)

  • Tiles follow the sport: five and More. A tile that holds more settings (trail colours, depths, rain, 3D) has a › side that opens them in the drawer.
  • Your own tiles: Edit beside the tiles, and a reset to the sport's tiles.
  • A sheet on a phone, closed with a swipe, with the same swipe as the planner's sheet (#96): one gesture for every sheet.
  • More opens the full panel as it is, with a way back; regrouping it waits for phase 21 (#119, #121).

1 · The quick drawer

Layers opens the drawer instead of the full panel: the base maps as thumbnails, five layers for your sport as tiles, and More for everything else. A tap changes the map at once and the drawer stays; ✕, a swipe down, a tap on the map or Layers again closes it.

2 · The settings behind a tile

A tile turns its layer on and off. When it holds more than one setting, its › side opens them in the drawer, one step in, with ‹ back: the same switches as its group in the full panel.

3 · With the planner open

The drawer is a moment's detour from planning: it never moves the planner or changes its size.

4 · Other sports, and dark

The tiles follow the sport; each turns the same switch as in the full panel, so what you choose is remembered per sport as today.

5 · Your own tiles

Edit, beside the tiles, swaps them for any switch in the full panel: five per sport, kept with your settings. Reset and Done sit at the bottom, in thumb reach; a swipe down is Done too.

6 · Behind More: the full panel

More, or a swipe up, grows the same sheet into the full panel; ‹ or a swipe down goes back to the drawer.

7 · The same swipe on the planner's sheet (#96)

Added in phase 24's review (2026-10-17), to try the one swipe on a real phone with four sizes: peek, profile, half and full. Drag from the handle, the header (also over its buttons), the tabs, or the list when it's scrolled to the top. The map's buttons move once, when the sheet comes to rest. Not the planner's real contents: only enough to feel the swipe.
