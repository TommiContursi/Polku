Map fill-in (#1091)

Way
Network
Frame

  • Try: Slow, As now, then drag or flick the map a screen or two: the new part is plain paper until its tiles come, and they come square by square. Go somewhere new and do the same with A.
  • A loads the map four zoom levels coarser for the area around the view whenever the map stops (1–4 tiles, each one sixteen times as wide). MapLibre already draws a coarser tile in the place of one that hasn't come yet; today the coarser one is seldom there in time. With A it is: lakes, sea, forest and the main roads are there at once, and the paths and buildings sharpen in.
  • B also loads the tiles at the map's own zoom half a screen beyond each edge, so a short move finds them ready. It costs about twice the tiles, most of them never looked at.
  • Nothing fades or moves on its own: the map is moved many times a minute, so it gets no motion (DESIGN.md, "Motion").
  • Left out: Polku's controls, overlays, hillshade, contours and trail details. The counts are this page's tiles only.
