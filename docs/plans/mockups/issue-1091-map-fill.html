  • Try: Slow, As now, then drag or flick the map a screen or two: the new part is plain paper until its tiles come, and they come square by square. Go somewhere new and do the same with A.
  • A keeps the map four zoom levels coarser under the view: 1–4 tiles, each one sixteen times as wide, so the same few cover a whole town and the area around it. MapLibre already draws a coarser tile it has in the place of one that hasn't come; today it seldom has one. With A it does: lakes, sea, forest and the main roads are there at once, and the paths, buildings and names sharpen in. The first time in a new region the coarse tiles come with the rest; after that they're there.
  • B also loads the tiles at the map's own zoom half a screen beyond each edge, so a short move finds them ready. It costs two to three times the tiles, three quarters of them never shown.
  • Nothing fades or moves on its own: the map is moved many times a minute, so it gets no motion (DESIGN.md, "Motion").
  • Left out: Polku's controls, overlays, hillshade, contours and trail details. The counts are this page's tiles only.
  • Try: Slow, As now, then go to a search result: the map flies there for 0.9 s and the place fills in after it lands. Then the same with each crossfade. Then go to a place nearby: it moves there in every version.
  • Polku as it is now under every version: the coarse map underneath (A), Finland's zoom-6 overview kept on the device (C), and Polku's own layers drawn together after the map stops (D). Here those layers are a stand-in: the paths again, in a trail colour, coming 0.35 s after the stop (the loaders' timers) and then the network's time.
  • Which jumps: a search result, a place, and Show the whole route, and only when the new view shares nothing with the old one. When they overlap the camera moves there as now, so you see where it went. A drag, a pinch, the wheel and the keys never crossfade.
  • Crossfade once drawn holds a picture of the old view while the new place's map is drawn under it, at most 0.4 s, then fades it out in 0.18 s. On a fast network you see the new place finished; on a slow one, after 0.4 s, what the coarse map has until the place's own tiles come: Finland's overview has lakes, sea and main roads, so in a forest that's paper for a moment. Polku's own layers come after it in their one change, as after any move.
  • Crossfade at once fades the old view out in 0.18 s straight away, and the new place fills in under it as it comes.
  • From the keys (Tab to a button, then Enter) nothing fades: once drawn still holds the old view, then shows the new one at once; at once just jumps. DESIGN.md: nothing started from the keyboard moves.
  • Reduced motion (the switch, or your device's setting): the flight becomes a jump, as MapLibre does today; the crossfade stays, as it's opacity only.
  • A touch, a click, the wheel or a key on the map while the old view shows drops it at once. The new place's names finish MapLibre's own 0.3 s fade after the crossfade.
  • Left out: Polku's controls, cards, markers and the route. The picture is of the map's canvas only, so markers and cards drawn in HTML aren't in it: to be checked in a build.
  • In this copy the tiles are part of the page: "As it is" holds each a third of a second the first time, as OpenFreeMap's took on a fast connection. Each place has its zoom 14 and 13 around it and the coarser map; the flight's middle shows Finland's overview only.
