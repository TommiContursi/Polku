A calmer route planner, with nothing taken away

Phase 24's mock-up, 2026-10-17. Not wired to the app: the map behind each frame is a screenshot of Polku with its controls hidden, and Today shows the real planner at 20f0310. The report is docs/reviews/2026-10-17-design/; the measurements under each frame come from evidence/mockup_probe.py.

Try it: switch between Today, A and B at the bottom; in B the routing mode, undo and redo float above the sheet. The waypoint rows have two ways too.

The rules every variant keeps (phase 24's plan)

  • Nothing Polku does is removed, and nothing used today takes more taps, more scrolling or a new hidden view.
  • One tap and in thumb reach while drawing: adding and moving points, undo, redo, the routing mode, Done.
  • Each thing shown once, where it's used; a control shows when it can do something; the map comes first.

1 · The start, before the first point

Today the peek shows thirteen controls before there's a route: the route's colour, undo, redo and Show the whole route can't do anything yet. The start choices (draw, a round trip, an import) are what this moment needs.

2 · Drawing: the peek

While drawing, the map comes first and the tools are at the thumb. The route's colour goes with the profile it colours; the sport is the top bar's pill (it shows the route's sport while planning, and re-routes as the planner's picker does); Show the whole route joins the map's buttons, shown while part of the route is out of view (it does nothing otherwise); the distance marks say the distance, and the times stay one switch away (Check › On the map).

3 · Checking: the sheet at half

One colour choice, with the profile it colours and "On map" beside it. Round trip once (in the header), the route style picked once (the header's chip; How you go keeps its settings). The waypoint rows: every row's five buttons at 40 px, or only the row you're on with all five at 44 px.

4 · Desktop: the side panel

One Done (the panel's), one colour choice, one sport control (the top bar's pill), Round trip and the route style once each. Show the whole route joins the map's buttons, under zoom and 3D (#226), while part of the route is out of view. The tools stay in the panel: on a desktop they're already under the pointer.
