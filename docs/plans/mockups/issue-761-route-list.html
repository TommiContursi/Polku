What Rows changes

  • A point is a row: its mark, its name, and one line: when you leave or get there, the weather then, a stop or a change of sport. Its distance from the start isn't on it (the stretch says how far). A tap opens its card (on a phone) or its popup (#760).
  • A plain stretch is a line between two points with its numbers in small type and ⌄ where it folds shaping points. A stretch that differs from a plain one (a straight line, over water, a LIPAS route or an imported track, a carry) has its icon and its word first, in the accent colour. A tap opens the stretch's card.
  • The sport's row heads its section: the sport, then its route style and its speed as values ("Balanced · Normal, 16 km/h"). A tap opens them. With two sports each section has its own row, and the point where the sport changes keeps its tag. How you go and Your speed leave the list's end.
  • Reorder sits under the list with Reverse, and Clear route on a row of its own under them (chosen on 2026-11-04; the switch shows it beside the sport's row, as today). On a desktop the grip reorders without it; the keyboard still needs it.
  • A desktop edits in the row: hover or focus a point's row and Rename and Remove appear where › was; the grip at its left drags it. On a phone the grip and the tools don't exist: the row opens the card, Reorder works as today.
  • Under the list: Reverse, and Clear route at the other end (chosen on 2026-11-04; the switch shows Reverse alone). Round trip, Back to start and Import are ways to begin or route actions: ⋯ and the start's card keep them.
  • The warning is in the key fact's slot under the numbers (#741), not a box in the list.

What Line changes (the second round)

  • One line runs from the start to the end through every waypoint's mark, as Komoot's does: each stretch is the line between two marks, green where it isn't along paths. Where the sport changes, the next sport's icon sits on the line as a node in its heading, so the parts read as one route.
  • The stretch's numbers are its first line, in bold, as before #780: "11.8 km · 38 min · ↑71 m". Under them, small: how it's drawn, with its icon, and the waypoints it folds.
  • How it's drawn on every stretch, "Follow paths" too, so a stretch along paths doesn't look like one with something missing; the ones that aren't along paths are green. The switch shows it only where it differs, as #780 does.
  • Said once: a part of one stretch has no numbers under its heading, as its stretch says them (PR 2 rebuilds the heading).
  • Waypoints: the points a stretch folds are "3 waypoints", never "shaping points". The list's other words that say "point" are in the plan.

What Parts changes (the third round)

  • Each sport has its colour: its part of the line and its icon at the head of the part are in the sport's colour, the one the map and the profile's strip already use for it (Colour by Sport); in dark mode a lighter one, for 3:1 on the dark glass. A route of one sport keeps the grey line.
  • Every part's heading looks the same, the first too: the sport's icon in a ring of its colour, on the line in the marks' column, then its name and its two ends. (Line had the ring only from the second part on, and the first heading looked like something else.)
  • The line breaks where the sport changes: it goes into the changeover point in one sport's colour, then dots lead past the rule between the parts to the next sport's icon. The changeover's own icon is in the next sport's colour too. Colour is never alone: the heading has the sport's icon and name, the point its tag.
  • A straight line is dashed, as on the map, and says so in words; a stretch that isn't along paths has its word in the text colour, bolder, not the accent's green.
  • Folded waypoints show on the line: a bead for each, three at most, and the words say which: "waypoints 1–5". The marks are numbered as on the map, so the point after five folded waypoints is 6.

Left out

  • The cards and the popup (#760), Reorder's own view, a night's row and a long route's folding (#350): not drawn.
  • Line keeps main's foot, How you go and Your speed, and the bottom dock's one column: the earlier PRs of the plan change those. Its unfolded waypoints aren't drawn; on the line they're marks like the others.
