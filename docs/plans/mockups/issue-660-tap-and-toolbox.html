One tap, one toolbox

What a tap on the map does while you plan, and where the planner's tools live. For #660, #690 and #233, 2026-11-03. Every frame is clickable: tap the map to add points or to open what's there, open the cards, move the sheet by its handle, use Undo. The switches at the top change every frame; Open under a frame shows it alone, at full size, which on a phone fills the screen. The plan, with the counts from the flows and the recommendation: issue-660-tap-and-toolbox.md.

Today is the app on main (f121d64): Edit and Done decide what a tap does, and the toolbox has five shapes for one route. Version 1 makes that state one named tool, Add points, and changes nothing else when it's off. Version 2 has no state: while the planner is open a tap on the map or a way adds a point, and Look makes the next tap open a card. The toolbox is the same five slots in every state, [the tool] [how the next leg is drawn · style] [undo] [redo] [⋯], in one of two places: (a) the sheet's own first row, at every size, or (b) a fixed column at the map's left.

The stretch's card, built from the pick's card

Today a stretch of your own route says its distance, time and climb; a stranger's path gets a profile with Colour by, the way, its surface, its difficulty and the weather (#690). Proposed: the stretch's card is the pick's card fed by the route's own analysis of that stretch, with the route's rows and actions added. The plan says which functions it takes.

Left out of the mock-up

  • Routing: a new leg runs straight from the last point to where you tapped (snapped to the way under it); the drawn route's three points are the fixture's own line. Distances, times and climbs of new legs are worked out from their length alone.
  • The sheet's spring (js/sheet.js): a swipe here moves one stop per swipe, and no motion is proposed. The toolbox's not moving at all is the point of both placements.
  • Route, Check and Go's contents beyond their first rows; the weather timeline and its layer; the profile's hover; the drawing menu's next-leg sport; Other ways' routing (a made-up other way comes for a new leg, with today's labels); round trips, Marked routes and imports behind the start's tiles; the 3D, Layers and Settings panels; dark mode on the screens.
  • The lit ways drawn by Show lit ways are a few of the map's own roads, coloured for the mock-up, not Lit ways' data.
  • Keyboard and screen readers are checked in the plan, not in the frames: the frames take a pointer.
