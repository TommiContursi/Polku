What's decided, and what isn't yet

  • Decided on 2026-11-21: a mark for each kind of barrier; coloured by who may pass: dark where nothing is mapped, red where it's closed (access=no or private, or locked=yes), green where it's open (access=yes, or open to bikes or people on foot); drawn with Access restrictions, not Trail details.
  • A closed mark also has a padlock, so it isn't told by its colour alone. An open one differs from an unmapped one by colour; its card says which.
  • Which kinds share a mark: a gate (gates, swing, sliding, wicket, hampshire and bump gates), a lift gate (lift gates and bars), a chain (chains and ropes), blocks, a stile (stiles, kissing gates and horse stiles), a turnstile (turnstiles of both heights), a cycle barrier (cycle and motorcycle barriers).
  • Chosen on 2026-11-21: B, the plain gate keeps today's posts and rails.
  • Not drawn here: whether a point is open to bikes or on foot, which Polku's trail details don't keep yet (#1232 adds bicycle and foot); the gates giving way at zoom 16 (#1221).
