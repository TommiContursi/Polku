The facts on the map's cards

What a tap on a trail, a road works sign or the water tells you. Each scene shows Today (as main draws it) beside A and B. The switches at the bottom right set the size (390 px, 360 px, a 390 phone at 200 %) and the worst case (long Finnish names, every fact the map can have).

Where it stands (2026-10-28): nothing is decided. Two choices: A or B for a trail's card and What's here (sections 1 and 2), and whether the map's other cards move onto the same card (section 3, the same in A and B). The plan, with what's left out, is docs/plans/issue-345-map-cards.md, "The facts on the cards (#561)".

A fixes the facts only: a trail's numbers become what the way is like for your sport (how hard, how steep, its surface), and What's here says when a detail wasn't loaded instead of "not mapped". B is A made calmer: What's here moves into the card's last row, where Not right? was, and Not right? moves into What's here, beside the data it's about. The "You're not editing" note becomes one line without its box (on all six cards that have it), and What's here lists what's known, then one line of what isn't.

1 · A trail's card

Today the three numbers are how far from your route, the height, and one fact for your sport (MTB: difficulty, Gravel, Road and Run: surface, Hike and Trail run: trail grade). The hover over the same trail says its MTB grade and how steep it is, and the card doesn't (#561). In A and B the numbers say what the way is like: your sport's own fact, the incline here (the hover's, worked out from the terrain), and the next fact the map has. A known fact comes before one that isn't mapped. How far from your route moves into the line under the name; the height moves to What's here, where it already is.

2 · What's here

The second level. In Gravel, Road, Run, Paddle and Swim, Polku doesn't load the trail details, so the card has only the base map's tags. Today every other row says "? not mapped", which is a claim about OpenStreetMap that isn't true (#562).

3 · The map's other cards

Road works, traffic incidents, road conditions, busy roads, military areas, sea marks and the water's depth still open the old popup, with its own look and actions: Route from here, Add to route and a red Avoid (#564). On the new card they get the map's own mark, their facts as rows, Your route (Start here, End here, Round trip from here, Avoid this spot) and the pill, as a trail's card has. Not right? stays off them: the data is Fintraffic's, Väylä's or Traficom's, not OpenStreetMap's. Dates outside this year get their year (#565). The values on the sea mark, the water and the busy road are examples; the road works are the ones seen on 2026-10-28.

What changes

  • A and B, a trail's numbers: your sport's fact, the incline, and the next fact the map has, known ones first. MTB: difficulty, incline, surface. Gravel and Road: surface, incline, difficulty. Hike and Trail run: trail grade, incline, surface. Run: surface, incline, trail grade. With Layers' difficulty colours on, the MTB grade is in every sport's list, since the map draws it. A third number that isn't mapped is left out; the first two say so in their place.
  • A and B, the line under the name: the hover's words that aren't in the numbers (0.3 m wide, faint in places, a bridge, private), then how far from your route.
  • A and B, What's here: a detail Polku didn't load says "not loaded", and All OpenStreetMap details asks for it. B folds the missing ones into one line under the facts.
  • B only: What's here › is in the card's last row (What's here ›, Share, Weather here ›), and Not right? is in What's here's last row, beside the data; "You're not editing" is one line without its box, on all six cards that have it; What's here lists only what's known, then one line of what isn't.
  • A and B, the other cards: on the map's card, with the year where it isn't this year. Ships and cameras keep their own live card and modal.
