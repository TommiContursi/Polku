The questions (the plan has each one’s cost)

  1. How the parts weigh. Recommended: the dark apart. The bars show the weather alone, minute for minute (minutes in rain, heavy rain double; feeling hot; lost to a headwind or strong wind on the water), the dark is shaded behind them, and the rows name the best in the light and the best in the dark (on a ten-hour ride, Most in the light: try Gravel, 10 hours). Or minute for minute: the dark in one sum with the weather, one ranking, and on a November weekday the rows always name the middle of the day (try November, after work).
  2. A day when every hour is bad. Recommended: name the least bad and say so, with a row for a better day (Every hour bad, Late evening). Or name nothing.
  3. When’s menu and Arrive by. Recommended: Best time to leave › is the menu’s last item (First day’s, with nights), and a pick is a plain Leave at, which takes Arrive by’s place. Or, while Arrive by is set, the bars also grey the departures that would get you there late.
  4. What rules an hour out. Recommended: the card’s dangers to life: thunder where you’d be, a frostbite risk, and waves over the craft’s comfort on the crossing (hatched, never named: Every hour bad with the sea kayak). Or nothing does.

Illustrative, or left out

  • Illustrative: every hour, number and word of the forecast, and the waves. The rule that makes them is the plan’s, run on made-up hours.
  • The weather under the new row is the card’s top half for the departure set: the sentence, one note, the numbers, the hours and the sections’ headings (folded; they don’t open here). The Whole trip, Hour by hour and Time on the way rows don’t open either. Weather on the map does nothing.
  • Not drawn: the map, the top bar (a stand-in), the planner beside the card on a desktop (a stand-in), the timeline, Check. A route of several sports (a line a sport in “For …”).
  • Nothing new moves (decided with /animate): the bars, ‹ › and a pick change in place, and the card opens as every card does. The skeleton while loading is drawn still.
  • The phone’s picker for Leave at… and Arrive by… isn’t drawn: in the menu here they set Sunday 10:00 and Arrive by 16:30.
