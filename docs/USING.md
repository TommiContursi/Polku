# Using Polku

[Open Polku](https://polku.si) · [Overview](../README.md)

## Plan and edit a route

Open **Plan**, choose a sport and add points on the map. Drag a point or the route line to change the plan. On a phone, hold the line before dragging it.

Select a part of the route to add a point there, change that leg's sport, routing mode or style, or ask for **Other ways**. After you add a point and stop for a moment, up to two other ways to it may appear on the map in blue, each labelled with how much longer or shorter it is and its share of trails; your route's label shows its own share, so you can compare. When you take your time between points they come together; otherwise the second can follow a few seconds later. Select one to take it, or carry on and the route stays as it is. After you take one, the way you had stays, grey, until your next point, so you can go back. **Other ways** in the menu shows up to two for any part. Selecting your route with the planner closed opens it again. Undo and redo let you try changes.

The planner has three tabs:

- **Route:** points, legs, round trips, styles and speeds.
- **Check:** warnings, climbs, ground conditions and route colours.
- **Go:** departure, travel time, weather, GPX export, sharing and offline save.

In **Check → On the route**, available colour kinds are grouped into **Terrain**, **Ground**, **Weather**, and **Route** for mixed sports. Groups sit side by side in a wide panel and stack on phones. Each kind has a bar, legend and distances. The title and bar form one compact button: tap either (**Use colours**) to select it in **Colour by** and colour the route on the map as well as the profile; the current choice says **Selected**. The legend below lines up category distances. Tap a group header to fold or open all its bars and legends together. Weather bars appear once the forecast arrives; kinds without data or that do not apply to the sport are left out.

With a mouse, **hover a bar segment or its legend row** to highlight every matching stretch on the map. This works even if another colouring is selected. Moving away clears the preview without changing your colours or moving the map. With a keyboard, Tab to a breakdown button: Left/Right steps through its categories, Home/End previews the first/last, and Escape or moving focus away clears the preview. Enter selects the colours as usual. Touch taps on the title or bar keep their colour-selection action and do not leave a hover preview.

In **Check → On the map**, distance marks, arrival times and elapsed times default on. Arrival is the clock time you reach a mark; elapsed time is the hours, minutes and seconds since departure, including stops and changeovers. Switch them off separately if needed; saved choices are kept. Tap the summary's time estimate to open **Go → Time** and focus its large **Depart** picker. It uses the same departure setting as the start waypoint and weather timeline.

The route glows on the map, and white chevrons drift along it the way it goes; they rest when you leave the map alone, and **Settings → Display → Moving arrows on the route** turns them off.

On a phone, drag the planner's handle to change how much of it is visible. On a desktop, the button at the end of the tabs moves the planner between the side and bottom of the map.

## Read the map

Open **Layers** for map layers and their keys. Trail difficulty uses mountain biking grades **S0–S6**; uphill difficulty uses **↑S0–↑S5**, with arrows pointing uphill. Select a trail for its details. On a desktop, resting the pointer on a feature gives a quick preview.

Search finds places, official LIPAS routes and stops, trails and coordinates. **Ride this route** puts an official route into the planner using its recorded line; **Add to route** joins it to your existing plan.

Route warnings appear under **Heads-up** in Check. **Avoid** asks the planner to route around that place. When a route goes round a way marked private, Heads-up says how much a short cut would save, and **Short cut** lets that leg through it (the leg's route style becomes **Ways marked private: Short cuts ok**). Use it only where you may go: private usually means a factory or military site, a house yard or a road closed by a sign, though in Finland private roads and apartment-block yards are open on foot and by bike. Weather along the route uses the estimated time you reach each section, so check your departure in Go and your speeds in Route.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcuts work when you are not typing in an input field.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| <kbd>T</kbd> | Toggle the weather timeline |
| <kbd>L</kbd> | Open layers and map keys |
| <kbd>R</kbd> | Open or close the route planner |
| <kbd>F</kbd> | Show the whole route |
| <kbd>/</kbd> | Focus search |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> | Undo |
| <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Z</kbd> | Redo |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Close open panels and menus |

## Map gestures

Pinching, or Ctrl+scrolling, over a panel zooms the map. In 3D, a two-finger trackpad scroll tilts and rotates; Shift+scroll tilts anywhere. You can also use the orbit pad, right-drag or a two-finger touch gesture.

## Before going offline

Use **Save offline** in Go or the planner's **⋯** menu. Then use **Preview offline** to check the saved coverage. On an iPhone, add Polku to the home screen to help keep the saved data available.

The saved map, terrain, route and profile work without a connection; routing, search, weather and traffic do not. Read the [limitations](../README.md#limitations), especially before planning on water.
